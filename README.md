Na **Secretaria de Inteligência Artificial do Piauí (SIA)** eu gerencio pesquisa e desenvolvimento de IA para o governo do estado. Na **Fábrica de Gênios**, que fundei e dirijo como CEO, desenvolvo produtos e formações em tecnologia. E sigo dando aula, em cursos livres, graduação e pós.

Most of my work is private: client, government or live products. This is a sample.

- **eProfessor:** SaaS where teachers upload their own PDFs and generate syllabi, activities, exams, slides and ebooks with AI.
- **LM3D:** AI-assisted 3D catalog, creation and editing, with a worker that repairs, decimates and converts meshes.
- **Prumo:** career map for tech people, combining adaptive technical testing, Big Five, DISC and situational judgment.
- **RAG drafting:** drafting and refining administrative rulings from real precedents, with a diff before applying.
- **Kairos:** devotional reading app with verse-based plans, streaks, notes and group study.
- **[Arena](https://github.com/ArturFelipeVeloso/AreanaSmart):** knockout tournament scoreboard, World Cup-style bracket, no build step.
